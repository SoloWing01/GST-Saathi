# Interest on Late Payment — Section 50

## What it means
Interest is levied under **Section 50 of the CGST Act, 2017** on any late payment of GST liability. It accrues automatically from the due date until the actual date of payment.

## Interest rates
- **Section 50(1)**: 18% per annum — on self-assessed tax paid after due date
- **Section 50(2)**: 24% per annum — on tax collected but not deposited (illegal collection)
- Interest is calculated on a daily basis on the outstanding tax amount
- Interest must be paid along with the tax — it cannot be deferred

## Common triggers
- Late payment of GST liability through GSTR-3B
- Short payment of tax discovered during self-assessment
- Excess ITC claimed and reversed later with interest
- Late payment of interest itself (compounding effect)
- Demand order specifying interest component

## Calculation method
- Interest = (Tax amount × 18% × number of days) / 365
- Calculated from the day after the due date to the actual date of payment
- Interest is on the NET tax liability (output tax minus ITC)
- If ITC is reversed later, interest runs from the original due date

## Response required
- Pay the interest along with the outstanding tax
- No separate notice response needed for self-assessed interest
- For demand-ordered interest, pay within 30 days or appeal
- Interest cannot be waived — it is a statutory levy

## Statutory reference
- **Section 50(1)** — Interest on late payment of self-assessed tax
- **Section 50(2)** — Interest on tax collected but not deposited
- **Section 50(3)** — Interest on excessive ITC claim
- **Rule 88A** — Recovery of interest
- **Section 73/74** — Interest component in demand orders

## Severity
Low-Medium — Interest is automatic and cannot be avoided. Paying on time prevents accrual. Once demanded, must be paid to avoid recovery proceedings.

# TDS/TCS Non-Compliance — Sections 51 & 52

## What it means
**Section 51 (TDS)** requires certain government entities to deduct 1% TDS when making payments to suppliers. **Section 52 (TCS)** requires e-commerce operators to collect 1% TCS on net taxable supplies. Non-compliance attracts notices and penalties.

## TDS under Section 51
- Applicable to: Government departments, local authorities, government agencies
- Rate: 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST, or 1% IGST)
- Threshold: Payment exceeds ₹2,50,000 per invoice
- Must be deposited by 10th of next month
- TDS return filed in GSTR-7 by 10th of next month

## TCS under Section 52
- Applicable to: E-commerce operators (marketplaces, aggregators)
- Rate: 1% (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST, or 1% IGST)
- On net taxable supplies (after adjustments)
- Must be deposited by 10th of next month
- TCS return filed in GSTR-8 by 10th of next month

## Common violations
- Non-deduction of TDS by government entities
- Non-collection of TCS by e-commerce operators
- Late deposit of TDS/TCS with the government
- Filing incorrect TDS/TCS returns
- Non-issuance of TDS/TCS certificates (GSTR-7A for TDS)

## Consequences
- Interest at 18% per annum on late deposit (Section 50)
- Penalty: ₹200/day for late filing of return (max ₹5,000)
- Penalty: ₹10,000 for non-deduction/non-collection
- Disallowance of expenditure for TDS default (under Income Tax Act)
- Recovery proceedings for unpaid TDS/TCS

## Statutory reference
- **Section 51** — Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)
- **Section 52** — Tax Collected at Source (TCS)
- **Rule 66** — TDS provisions
- **Rule 67** — TCS provisions
- **Section 50** — Interest on late payment
- **Section 122** — Penalty for non-compliance

## Severity
Medium — For e-commerce sellers, TCS means your tax is already collected — you just need to reconcile. For government contractors, TDS non-compliance can block payments.

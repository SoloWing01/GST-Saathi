# GSTR-1 Late Filing — Late Fee Notice

## What it means
A notice for late filing of GSTR-1 (outward supply return) under **Section 47 of the CGST Act, 2017**. Late fees accrue automatically for each day of delay.

## Common triggers
- Late filing of monthly GSTR-1 (due 11th of next month)
- Late filing of quarterly GSTR-1 under QRMP scheme (due 13th of month after quarter)
- Incomplete or incorrect return requiring amendment
- Technical issues on GST portal during filing

## Late fee calculation
- **GSTR-1 (monthly)**: ₹50/day, max 0.5% of turnover in the state
- **GSTR-1 (quarterly QRMP)**: ₹25/day for nil return, ₹50/day otherwise
- Late fee is per return — each period has its own late fee
- Late fee is automatically calculated by the system

## Impact of late GSTR-1 filing
- Your recipients cannot claim ITC on your invoices (no GSTR-2B auto-population)
- Your business compliance rating is affected
- Repeated late filing may trigger scrutiny under Section 61
- Blocking of e-way bill generation if returns not filed for 2 consecutive periods

## Response required
- File the pending GSTR-1 immediately
- Pay the late fee along with the return
- No separate reply needed — filing + payment resolves the matter
- If the late fee amount is disputed, can be contested at appeal stage

## Statutory reference
- **Section 37** — Furnishing details of outward supplies (GSTR-1)
- **Section 47** — Levy of late fee
- **Rule 59** — Manner of furnishing details of outward supplies
- **Notification 14/2020** — Late fee reduction for certain periods

## Severity
Low — Late fees are automatic and cannot be waived. Filing is the only remedy.

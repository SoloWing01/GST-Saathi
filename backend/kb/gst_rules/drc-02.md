# DRC-02 — Reply to Show Cause Notice

## What it means
DRC-02 is the **reply** you file in response to a Show Cause Notice (DRC-01). It is filed by the taxpayer to contest, explain, or accept the allegations made in the SCN.

## When it applies
- After receiving DRC-01 (Show Cause Notice)
- Within 30 days of receiving the SCN (extendable to 30+ days in some cases)
- Filed electronically through the GST portal

## Filing process
- Login to GST portal → Services → Lections → Orders → DRC-02
- Select the reference number of the SCN you are replying to
- Upload supporting documents and evidence
- Submit the reply electronically

## Key elements of a good reply
- Point-by-point response to each allegation in the SCN
- Legal arguments citing relevant sections and rulings
- Documentary evidence (invoices, bank statements, reconciliations)
- Calculation of actual tax liability vs. alleged liability
- Request for personal hearing if complex matters are involved

## Statutory reference
- **Section 73(6)** — Reply to SCN under Section 73
- **Section 74(6)** — Reply to SCN under Section 74
- **Rule 142(1)** — Manner of reply to SCN
- **Section 116** — Personal hearing provisions

## Outcome possibilities
- Officer accepts reply → proceedings dropped, no demand
- Officer partially accepts → reduced demand
- Officer rejects reply → final demand order (DRC-05 or DRC-07)
- Taxpayer can appeal to First Appellate Authority (Section 107)

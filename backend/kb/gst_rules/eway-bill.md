# E-Way Bill Non-Compliance

## What it means
E-Way Bill is mandatory for movement of goods exceeding ₹50,000 in value under **Section 68 of the CGST Act, 2017** and **Rule 138 of CGST Rules**. Non-generation, incorrect generation, or non-cancellation of e-way bills can attract penalties and notices.

## Common triggers
- Movement of goods without a valid e-way bill
- E-way bill generated with incorrect details (wrong GSTIN, wrong HSN, wrong value)
- E-way bill not cancelled within 24 hours of goods not being moved
- E-way bill expired (validity not extended for long-distance)
- Goods found in transit without accompanying e-way bill
- Mismatch between e-way bill details and GSTR-1 details

## Penalty provisions
- **Section 129**: Detention, seizure, and release of goods
  - Penalty: 200% of tax payable or ₹10,000, whichever is higher (for evading tax)
  - For other cases: ₹10,000 or amount specified in the order
- **Section 122(1)(xiv)**: ₹10,000 penalty for transporting goods without proper documents
- Goods can be released on payment of applicable penalty

## Response required
- Generate e-way bill before movement of goods
- Ensure all details match with GSTR-1 and invoice
- Cancel e-way bill if goods are not moved within 24 hours
- Extend validity if transit takes longer than expected
- Carry invoice, e-way bill, and delivery challan during transit

## Statutory reference
- **Section 68** — Inspection of goods in movement
- **Rule 138** — E-way bill generation
- **Rule 138A** — Documents and devices
- **Rule 138B** — Verification of documents
- **Section 129** — Detention, seizure, release
- **Section 122** — Penalty for certain offences

## Severity
Medium-High — Goods can be detained/seized on road. 200% penalty is severe. Prevention (generating correct e-way bills) is far cheaper than cure.

# REG-17 — Show Cause Notice for Cancellation of Registration

## What it means
REG-17 is a notice issued under **Section 29(2) of the CGST Act, 2017** proposing to cancel your GST registration. It gives you an opportunity to show cause why your registration should not be cancelled.

## Common triggers
- Non-filing of returns for a continuous period (6 months or more for quarterly filers, 3 months for monthly filers)
- Non-commencement of business within 6 months of registration
- Violation of anti-profiteering provisions
- Availing ITC fraudulently or using registration for tax evasion
- Furnishing false information at the time of registration
- Not maintaining business premises as declared

## Typical response required
- File a reply within 30 days via DRC-06 explaining why registration should not be cancelled
- Submit all pending returns (even nil returns)
- Provide proof of business operations if non-commencement is alleged
- Show cause against fraud allegations with evidence
- Request personal hearing for complex cases

## Statutory reference
- **Section 29(2)** — Cancellation of registration
- **Section 29(2)(c)** — Non-filing of returns for continuous period
- **Section 29(2)(e)** — Violation of anti-profiteering provisions
- **Rule 21** — Registration to be cancelled in certain cases
- **Rule 22** — Procedure for cancellation of registration

## Severity
Very High — Loss of GST registration means you cannot collect GST, claim ITC, or file returns. It effectively stops your business's GST compliance.

## Key points for merchants
- Filing all pending returns is the quickest way to prevent cancellation
- Even after cancellation, you must file final returns (GSTR-10)
- Cancellation can be revoked if applied within the specified time
- Outstanding tax liabilities survive cancellation — you still must pay

# ASMT-10 — Scrutiny of Returns

## What it means
ASMT-10 is a notice issued under **Section 61 of the CGST Act, 2017** when the proper officer finds discrepancies between the details furnished in your GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) and the information available on the GST portal or from other sources.

## Common triggers
- Mismatch between outward supply details reported in GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B
- Difference in ITC claimed in GSTR-3B vs ITC available as per GSTR-2B
- Variation in tax paid vs tax payable based on auto-populated data
- Inconsistencies in input tax credit availed across periods
- Suppression of outward supplies or over-claiming of ITC

## Typical response required
- File a reply within 30 days of receiving the notice
- Explain the discrepancies with supporting documents
- Rectify the returns if errors are accepted
- Provide reconciliations of GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B figures
- Submit supporting invoices, payment records, and bank statements

## Statutory reference
- **Section 61** — Scrutiny of returns
- **Section 61(1)** — Proper officer may scrutinize returns and intimate discrepancies
- **Rule 59** — Procedure for scrutiny of returns
- **Section 73/74** — If discrepancies lead to tax demand proceedings

## Severity
Medium — This is an inquiry notice, not a demand. Response is mandatory to avoid ex-parte proceedings.

## Key points for merchants
- This is NOT a demand notice yet — it's asking for an explanation
- Failing to respond can lead to ex-parte proceedings under Section 61(6)
- The officer may accept your explanation and close the matter
- If not satisfied, they may initiate demand proceedings under Section 73 or 74

# ITC Reversal — Section 17(5) Blocked Credits

## What it means
Certain Input Tax Credits are **blocked** under **Section 17(5) of the CGST Act, 2017**. If you have claimed ITC on these blocked credits, you must reverse the ITC with interest.

## Common blocked credits (Section 17(5))
- Motor vehicles and conveyances (with exceptions)
- Food and beverages, outdoor catering, beauty treatment, health services, cosmetic/plastic surgery (unless used for further supply or mandatory under law)
- Membership of club, health, fitness center
- Works contract services for construction of immovable property (other than plant and machinery)
- Goods/services for construction of immovable property on own account
- Goods/services on which tax is paid under composition scheme
- Personal consumption goods
- Goods lost, stolen, destroyed, written off, or given as free samples/gifts
- Tax paid due to fraud, collusion, suppression, etc. (Section 74)

## Typical response required
- Identify ITC claimed on blocked credits
- Reverse the ITC in the next return (GSTR-3B)
- Pay interest at 18% under Section 50(3) from date of availing ITC
- Adjust against output tax liability if possible
- Provide details in the return if questioned

## Statutory reference
- **Section 17(5)** — Blocked credits list
- **Section 16(2)** — Conditions for ITC (must be used for business purpose)
- **Section 50(3)** — Interest on ineligible ITC
- **Rule 42** — Reversal of ITC for exempt/non-business use
- **Rule 43** — Reversal of ITC for capital goods

## Severity
Medium — Reversal is mandatory with interest. Non-reversal may lead to demand proceedings. Self-assessment reversal avoids notices.

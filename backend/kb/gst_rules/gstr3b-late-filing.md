# GSTR-3B Late Filing — Late Fee and Interest

## What it means
A notice for late filing of GSTR-3B (monthly/quarterly summary return) under **Sections 47 and 50 of the CGST Act, 2017**. Late fees and interest accrue for each day of delay in filing and/or paying tax.

## Common triggers
- Late filing of GSTR-3B (due 20th of next month for monthly, 22nd/24th for quarterly)
- Late payment of tax liabilities in GSTR-3B
- Nil return not filed by due date
- Technical difficulties during filing period

## Late fee and interest calculation
- **Late fee**: ₹50/day (₹25/day for nil returns), max 0.5% of turnover
- **Interest**: 18% per annum on net tax liability from due date to payment date
- Interest is calculated on the tax amount, not on late fee
- Both late fee and interest are auto-calculated by the portal

## Consequences of repeated late filing
- Trigger for scrutiny notice under Section 61
- Potential cancellation of registration under Section 29(2)(c) for continuous non-filing
- Blocking of e-way bill generation
- ITC reversal required for recipients
- Affects compliance rating

## Response required
- File GSTR-3B immediately with correct tax liability
- Pay late fee and interest along with the return
- No separate reply to the notice is needed
- If compliance is regularized, the matter is considered resolved

## Statutory reference
- **Section 39** — Furnishing returns (GSTR-3B)
- **Section 47** — Levy of late fee
- **Section 50** — Interest on late payment
- **Section 50(1)** — Interest at 18% on net tax liability
- **Rule 61** — Manner of filing GSTR-3B

## Severity
Low-Medium — Regular filing resolves the issue. But persistent non-filing escalates to scrutiny and potential cancellation.

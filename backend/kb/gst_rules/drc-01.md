# DRC-01 — Show Cause Notice (Tax Demand)

## What it means
DRC-01 is a **Show Cause Notice (SCN)** issued under **Section 73(1) or Section 74(1) of the CGST Act, 2017**. It formally alleges that you have short-paid or evaded GST and gives you an opportunity to explain before a demand is raised.

## Common triggers
- Short payment of tax compared to what the department calculates
- Unlawful availment or utilization of Input Tax Credit (ITC)
- Excess refund claimed or received
- Non-payment or short payment of tax due to fraud, collusion, suppression, or misstatement
- Discrepancies identified during audit or investigation
- Mismatch between GSTR-1 and GSTR-2B for the recipient

## Typical response required
- File a reply within 30 days via GST DRC-06
- Contest the allegations with evidence and legal arguments
- Pay the disputed tax (with interest) if the demand is accepted
- Request a personal hearing if needed
- Provide documentary evidence for ITC claims

## Statutory reference
- **Section 73(1)** — Demand for short payment/non-payment (without fraud)
- **Section 74(1)** — Demand involving fraud/collusion/suppression
- **Section 73(9)** — Time limit: 3 years from due date of return
- **Section 74(9)** — Time limit: 5 years from due date of return
- **Section 16(2)** — Conditions for availing ITC
- **Rule 36(4)** — ITC restricted to 105% of eligible credit in GSTR-2B

## Severity
High — This is a formal demand notice. Non-response leads to ex-parte demand order (DRC-05/DRC-07).

## Key points for merchants
- You MUST respond within 30 days — silence = ex-parte proceedings
- The demand becomes final if no reply is filed (DRC-07 order)
- You can pay the tax and interest to settle, or contest with evidence
- A late fee of ₹10,000 or 10% of tax (whichever is higher) may apply under Section 74(10) if you lose

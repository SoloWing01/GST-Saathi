# GSTR-3A — Notice for Non-Filing of Returns

## What it means
GSTR-3A is a **statutory reminder notice** issued under **Section 46 of the CGST Act, 2017** when you fail to file your GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9) by the due date.

## Common triggers
- Failure to file GSTR-1 (outward supplies) by the due date
- Failure to file GSTR-3B (summary return) by the due date
- Failure to file GSTR-9 (annual return) by the due date
- Failure to file CMP-08 (composition taxpayers) by the due date
- Persistent non-filing across multiple periods

## Consequences of ignoring GSTR-3A
- Late fee of ₹50/day for GSTR-3B (₹25/day for nil returns), capped at 0.5% of turnover
- Late fee of ₹25/day for GSTR-1 (₹10/day for nil returns), capped at 0.5% of turnover
- Blocking of e-way bill generation
- Blocking of ITC claiming for the recipient (if supplier doesn't file GSTR-1)
- Potential cancellation of GST registration under Section 29(2)(c)
- Interest on late payment of tax (18% per annum under Section 50)

## Response required
- File all pending returns immediately
- Pay applicable late fees and interest
- No formal reply is needed — this is a system-generated notice
- Compliance is the only remedy

## Statutory reference
- **Section 46** — Failure to furnish returns
- **Section 47** — Levy of late fee
- **Section 50** — Interest on late payment
- **Rule 88A** — Recovery of interest or penalty
- **Rule 22** — Registration cancellation for non-filing

## Severity
Low-Medium — This is a compliance reminder. Immediate action (filing returns) resolves it. But continued ignoring escalates to registration cancellation.
